# Privacy Policy - RaceStats / Live Race Stats

_Last updated: 2026-07-10_

RaceStats is a Garmin Connect IQ data field that shows a rider's live race
standing during a race.

## What the app sends

To fetch your standings, the app makes a read-only web request to the timing
site configured in its settings (by default `https://universalbicycle.team`),
using two values **you enter yourself**:

- the **competition id** of the race, and
- your **bib number**.

That request returns the public race standings for that bib (place, time gaps,
laps) and the app displays them. The bib number and competition id are race
identifiers, not personal information.

## What the app does NOT do

- It does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data - no name, no
  email, no location, no heart rate, no activity or fitness data.
- It does **not** upload anything from your device; every request is read-only.
- It contains **no** analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- It shares nothing with any third party other than the timing site you
  configure, and only the competition id and bib you enter.

## Data storage

The most recently fetched standings are cached on the device so the field can
render them, and are overwritten on each refresh. Nothing is stored off the
device by the app.
